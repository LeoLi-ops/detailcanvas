Frequently Asked Questions

Are my photos uploaded to the cloud or external servers?

Never. DetailCanvas processes all images 100% locally on your iPhone using the Apple Neural Engine (ANE) and Metal GPU. Your pictures never leave your device, and the app works completely offline.

What is the difference between "Ultra Clarity" and "Natural Smooth" modes?

Ultra Clarity leverages the Apple Neural Engine for maximum sharpness, reconstructing crisp high-frequency micro-textures (ideal for portraits, hair, and close-ups). Natural Smooth provides smooth gradients and stable transitions (ideal for complex landscapes or wide-angle photography).

How do I restore my Pro subscription on a new device?

Open the side drawer menu in DetailCanvas, tap the Pro upgrade banner, and tap "Restore purchases" at the bottom of the paywall. Ensure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used to make the original purchase.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Open your iPhone Settings app > tap your Apple Account profile at the top > tap Subscriptions > select DetailCanvas Pro > tap Cancel Subscription.

How does the Private Photo Vault work?

The Private Photo Vault stores photos in an isolated app sandbox protected by 256-bit encryption. The encryption key is kept inside your iOS Keychain, and unlocking the vault requires biometric verification (Face ID or Touch ID).

Why did my upscale fail or take longer on an older image?

Deep neural network upscaling requires substantial computational power. Very large input images are automatically tiled or safety-scaled to prevent device memory exhaustion. If you encounter any issue, try switching between Ultra Clarity and Natural Smooth modes.
