Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 24, 2026

Summary: DetailCanvas is designed with privacy at its core. All photo enhancement and processing run 100% locally on your device. We do not upload, collect, store, or transmit your photos or personal data to any external server.

1. 100% On-Device Image Processing

DetailCanvas uses on-device artificial intelligence powered by the Apple Neural Engine (ANE) and Metal GPU. When you select, view, or enhance an image:

  • Your photos never leave your device.
  • No image data is ever uploaded to our servers or any third-party cloud.
  • Processing works entirely offline, even in Airplane Mode without an internet connection.

2. Photo Library Access

DetailCanvas requests permission to access your iOS Photo Library strictly for the following functional reasons:

  • Read Access: To let you pick photos you wish to enlarge or restore.
  • Add Access: To allow you to save your enhanced images back into your Photos app.

We do not scan your photo library, read metadata unrelated to your chosen photos, or build any profile from your pictures.

3. Private Photo Vault & Security

DetailCanvas includes an optional Private Vault feature. Photos imported into the vault are stored in an encrypted sandbox directory on your device. The encryption key is protected by the iOS Keychain, and access is gated by your device's biometric authentication (Face ID, Touch ID, or Passcode). We have zero technical means to view or recover your vaulted images.

4. Data Collection & Analytics

We believe user tracking is unnecessary. DetailCanvas:

  • Contains zero third-party tracking SDKs or advertising networks.
  • Does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, or device identifiers.
  • Does not sell, rent, or monetize user data in any form.

5. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

All in-app purchases and subscriptions are handled directly through Apple's StoreKit 2 framework. Apple processes your payments securely under its own privacy policy. DetailCanvas only verifies cryptographically signed transaction receipts provided by iOS on your device to unlock Pro features. We never see or store your credit card or billing details.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
